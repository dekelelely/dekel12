The concepts

Each in color, reverse, and one color.

Concept 1

Swallow Bridge

Dror means both “swallow” and “freedom”. The swallow’s wings make an arch, a bridge carried by a bird that crosses borders every year, and the gold line beneath is the way across. It continues the swallow in the school’s current logo, lighter and moving forward.

Concept 2

Flame & Sprout

A memorial flame with a young sprout growing from its heart: remembrance that makes room for growth. The sprout is cut out of the flame, so the two are one shape. The quietest of the four, set in the serif Frank Ruhl Libre, with the line “remembering the past, building the future” underneath.

Wording

  • קריית חינוך דרור
  • משלחת לגרמניה 2026
  • זוכרים את העבר · בונים את העתיד

Frank Ruhl Libre, tagline in Assistant. Hebrew only. Files in 2-flame-sprout/

Concept 3

ד meets D

The Hebrew ד of Dror and the Latin D face each other under one roofline: two alphabets, one name. The roof of the ד reaches toward the D and stops a hairline short, cut to the curve of its neighbour, so they meet without merging. This is the one concept that carries the German line.

Wording

  • קריית חינוך דרור
  • משלחת לגרמניה 2026
  • Delegation nach Deutschland 2026

Heebo. Hebrew + German. Files in 3-dalet-d/

Concept 4

Two Cities, One Line

A Jerusalem stone gate and citadel on one side, the Reichstag’s glass dome and Berlin’s TV tower on the other, joined by a bridge drawn in the same single line. The gold sun over the bridge is light over both. The avatar version keeps one landmark per city.

Wording

  • מסע לגרמניה 2026
  • Dror Educational Campus · Delegation to Germany 2026

Assistant. Hebrew + English. Files in 4-two-cities/
