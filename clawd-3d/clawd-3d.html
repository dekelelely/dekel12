In the terminal, Clawd is three lines of block characters. Each character splits into four quadrants, so the drawing is an 18 × 6 grid of cells about twice as tall as they are wide. The model places its eyes, arms and four legs on the same columns, and takes its depth and proportions from the 3D-printed figure: a body about 4:3, square eyes raised off the face, arms at mid-height.
